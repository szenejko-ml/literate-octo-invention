# Proposed manuscript-form structure for the unpublished doctoral analysis

"
"This is ONE self-contained manuscript-like study, not four separate papers. The Results are divided into four analytic modules.

"
"## Title
"
"Reassurance-oriented three-zone first-trimester chromosomal screening: nested validation, clinical reclassification and explainability audit

"
"## Structured abstract
Background / Objective / Methods / Results / Conclusions.

"
"## 1. Introduction
"
"Clinical problem; limits of threshold-only trisomy-oriented screening; rationale for an euploidy/reassurance-oriented decision framework; distinction between screening reassurance and diagnostic confirmation.

"
"## 2. Methods
"
"2.1 Study design and participants
"
"2.2 Outcome/reference standard
"
"2.3 Predictors and clinical comparators
"
"2.4 Models and preprocessing
"
"2.5 Nested validation, calibration and two-threshold three-zone policy
"
"2.6 Primary performance measures and confidence intervals
"
"2.7 Secondary subgroup and reclassification analyses
"
"2.8 Exploratory error-structure and XAI analyses
"
"2.9 Surrogate-tree fidelity analysis
"
"2.10 Sensitivity analyses and independent clinical-indication audit
"
"2.11 Reporting and quality frameworks

"
"## 3. Results
"
"### Module A - Primary locked analysis: overall OOF performance and two thresholds
"
"Thresholds by outer fold; final frozen thresholds; absolute counts; 95% CIs; discrimination and calibration; CST/CST+/logistic benchmarks.

"
"### Module B - Clinical operating consequences
"
"Age <=34 vs >=35; baseline CST and CST+ groups (<1:1000, 1:300-1:1000, 1:100-1:300, >1:100); T21/T18/T13/45,X/other; paired reclassification and absolute gains/losses.

"
"### Module C - Error structure and explainability
"
"Euploid non-low and non-euploid low analysed separately; overlap/Jaccard and paired comparisons; SHAP for selected source models; local audit of all false reassurance; surrogate-tree fidelity.

"
"### Module D - Robustness/sensitivity
"
"Predictor-complete cohort; common-record sensitivity; effect of anomaly/CHD override; NT >=3.5 mm/independent clinical-attention audit; year-specific results descriptive only.

"
"## 4. Discussion
"
"Main findings; euploidy-first paradigm; comparison with three constituent papers; interpretation of XAI; clinical role; external/prospective validation needed.

"
"## 5. Limitations
"
"Internal validation; small 2020/2023 strata; possible repeat pregnancies; outcome-verification limitations; post-hoc status of subgroup/XAI extension; OOF-dependence of uncertainty estimates; cloud TabPFN reproducibility limitations.

"
"## 6. Conclusions
"
"No deployment claim. State what the locked analysis demonstrates and what remains to be externally validated.

"
"Primary reporting framework: TRIPOD+AI.
"
"Supplementary cross-checks: STARD-AI (diagnostic-accuracy elements only), STROBE (retrospective cohort/data-source elements), PROBAST+AI (quality/risk-of-bias/applicability audit; not a reporting checklist), FUTURE-AI (fairness/traceability/usability/robustness/explainability/clinical translation).
